# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML + CSS + a little JS, no build step, published by GitHub Pages from this repository (client's choice, 2026-11-03: "like ecoes.eu"; edits go through Claude, push → live). Forms are sent by FormSubmit (no backend), as on ecoes.eu. Planned address: https://franchise.ecoes.eu (DNS at SuperHosting, not set up yet).

## Users

- Prospective franchisees: entrepreneurs and investors who want their own mobile energy business, with one ECOES MultiCharger or a network of them, in Europe, Africa, Asia and Latin America.
- Hotels, motels, petrol stations, roadside restaurants and other roadside businesses (backup power plus charging revenue, LPG/PNG option).
- They arrive to judge whether the business is real and safe (certification, numbers, support) and to send a pre-order or ask for an offer.

## Product Purpose

The site presents ECOES Franchise, the turnkey platform of ECOES JV for mobile EV charging, and its two products, and collects pre-orders (sales start in 2027, after certification) and offer requests by e-mail.

## Positioning

"We do not compete with stationary charging stations – we replace them where they are not." A certified mobile DC fast-charging station (diesel, LPG or PNG generator) that goes where the grid has no capacity, with vending, WC and cloud billing built in; the franchise sells a working, certified business, not an idea.

## Operating Context

- ECOES MultiCharger: 100 kW DC (1 × 100, 2 × 50, 3 × 33.3 kW), on a trailer (pulled by a pickup, category B+E) or a truck, three sections (power, customer service with vending and WC, operator room), −35 … +45 °C, AC 230/400 V 50/60 Hz output, EU Stage II/III or V generator, card/QR payments into the franchisee's bank account, remote monitoring, two road signs, shipped in a 20 ft (one) or 40 ft (two) container.
- ECOES MultiCharger Box: budget version, two DC chargers, 60 kW (1 × 60, 2 × 30), on a small truck, pickup or trailer supplied by the franchisee, about 2× cheaper, backup power 230/400 V 60 kW for sites, LPG (with 5 000/10 000 l tank and LPG pump) or PNG (methane) for stationary use, shipped in a container.
- Profitability is given by the client for Europe (diesel 2.0 EUR/l, 0.60 EUR/kWh production, 1.20 EUR/kWh mobile, 0.90 EUR/kWh stationary): tables at 10–100% load, payback "about 5 months" at 50% load (MultiCharger) and for the Box. The client marks them as indicative.
- Pre-order form (8 fields): applicant; phone/WhatsApp, e-mail, website; country, city, postal code; type; fuel (diesel, LPG, PNG); quantity; emission level (EU Stage II/III or V); mobility (trailer or truck). Consent: "I agree that ECOES JV may use the data in this form to answer my inquiry." No cookie banner (no analytics, no ads).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages (from the client's visualisation PDF): Home, About us (content from ecoes.eu/about-us/), ECOES Franchise, ECOES MultiCharger, ECOES MultiCharger Box, Contact us (content from ecoes.eu/contact-us/).
- Languages: English (root) and Bulgarian (`bg/`), client's choice 2026-11-03. Home and the form are the client's English; the other pages are the client's Bulgarian, English is our translation. Any other language through the browser's own translation.
- "Get offer" link on every product link, to the contact form (as "Get a quote" on ecoes.eu).
- Terminology: ECOES Franchise, ECOES MultiCharger, ECOES MultiCharger Box (Latin, never translated), франчайзополучател (franchisee).

## Brand Commitments

- The ECOES JV logo exactly as on ecoes.eu (`assets/img/logo.svg`); nothing else of the ecoes.eu look is binding (client: "only the logo is the same").
- Client's brief (2026-11-03): professional, modern, futuristic but true to the subject, clean, trustworthy, must not look AI-made; transparent containers with visible motifs; the strongest points and reasons on Home as linked containers, each with a "get offer" link. The client's visualisation colours (#C8161D red, #1D2227 dark, #F3F5F7 light grey) may be used; the design choice was delegated.
- Client's feedback on v1 (2026-11-03): rejected the red equipment-body design ("made in Paint", "all red strains the eye", "too obvious"); wants it nonchalant and mysterious while professional, modern, futuristic and clean; much more transparency: blurred glass containers with motifs visible behind them. References they like: an eco dashboard on a glass monitor with a glowing globe and leaves, a dark engineering site with a glowing wireframe building, a cinematic dark car page.
- Slogan in English changed at the client's request (2026-11-03): "We do not compete with stationary charging stations. We replace them where they are absent." (was "…where they are not").
- The client's texts are used verbatim; obvious typos are fixed and reported.

## Product Principles

1. Prove with the product: real renders, real specifications, the client's own numbers.
2. Trust before persuasion: certification, engineering detail and honest caveats stay visible.
3. Every path ends in an action: pre-order or get an offer, never a dead end.
4. Translation-proof: long Bulgarian and translated words must never break a layout.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Contrast ≥ 4.5:1, visible focus, 44 px targets, reduced motion respected, no horizontal scroll 320–1920 px, works with browser translation (as on ecoes.eu).
