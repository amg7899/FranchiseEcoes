I. What is ECOES Franchise

ECOES Franchise is a complete turnkey platform for mobile charging infrastructure that combines proven industrial engineering, autonomous management and a centralised digital ecosystem. With industrial hardware, full certification and cloud management united in one charging process, a mobile energy business with a predictable and fully autonomous source of revenue can be started and scaled from day one.

All payments for charging and for customer services go through the payment system of ECOES Franchise, through which the money goes straight into the franchisee’s bank account.

1. The franchise model is delivered through:

  1. Unified technological design

    The same model of charging station, protected as a registered design at the European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO), the same engine, thermodynamic ventilation and a 3-port DC charging system with zero defects.

  2. Legal and regulatory shield

    Type approval, IEC/CE safety marking and certified DC energy meters (MID), valid worldwide.

  3. Automated operational management

    Cloud billing, POS payments, collection of local VAT and remote telemetry (FOTA), all running on their own, with no need for an accounts department at each charging station.

Sales of the ECOES MultiCharger will begin in 2027, following the completion of the certification process.

2. The full certification process includes the five main certification pillars:

The five main certification pillars

  1. Automotive standard

    Type approval for full compliance with EU and UN requirements for bodywork and rescue vehicles (ready European CoC for immediate registration).

  2. Machinery Directive

    Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230.

    EN ISO 8528-13: the specific safety standard for generating sets driven by internal combustion engines.

  3. Energy standard

    Charging architecture under LVD 2014/35/EU:

    EN IEC 61851-1: conductive charging systems for electric vehicles.

    EN IEC 61851-23: specific requirements for DC charging stations for electric vehicles.

    EN IEC 61851-24: digital communication between the charging station and the vehicle (protocols ISO 15118 and DIN 70121).

  4. Electromagnetic compatibility (EMC)

    EMC Directive 2014/30/EU:

    EN 61000-6-2 / EN 61000-6-4: immunity and emission of interference in industrial environments.

  5. Outdoor noise level

    Outdoor Noise Directive (2000/14/EC): the guaranteed sound power level.

Additional standards and certificates

  1. Metrology standard

    Certified commercial metering (MID class), allowing the legal sale of energy and the issue of tax invoices.

  2. Industrial maintenance standard

    Equipment from world leaders (Perkins, Stamford, Leroy-Somer and others in modular power electronics), which ensures access to spare parts and service anywhere on the planet.

  3. Quality standard

    Company certification to ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001, covering the production and service of mobile charging stations.

  4. Certified module as one indivisible unit (Single Assembly)

    CB scheme (IECEE CB Scheme).

II. Why ECOES Franchise

  • A ready certified asset instead of years of development
  • Hardware, software and remote support
  • Mobile, stationary or mixed mode
  • What does the franchisee actually buy and sell?

A ready certified asset instead of years of development

In business, creating a new asset is a difficult task, but making that asset work and generate profits is even harder. That is why ECOES Franchise was created: to enable its franchisees to acquire a complex asset that generates substantial and predictable profits, in a complex and strictly regulated activity that brings together automotive and power electrical engineering.

State regulations and supervisory authorities do not allow a public service to be offered without strict control of the certification and safety of the products and services sold. That is why electricity as a product and electric-vehicle charging as a service can only be sold through the certified product of ECOES Franchise – the ECOES MultiCharger.

Designing, manufacturing and certifying safety-related products takes months, even years. It also takes substantial financial resources – tens and hundreds of thousands of EUR/USD. By joining the ECOES Franchise family you get a ready certified product that starts making a profit for you from the first day you take it over.

Hardware, software and remote support

ECOES Franchise supplies not only the hardware but also the software that keeps the ECOES MultiCharger running normally, and the payment system that issues the invoices and pays the money into your bank account.

In practice, software problems are the easiest to arise and the hardest to fix. In electrical machines with complex electronics about 80% of failures are software failures. ECOES Franchise detects and fixes software faults remotely, to keep your charging units running without trouble.

Mobile, stationary or mixed mode

The ECOES MultiCharger can work as a mobile charging station, and as a stationary one wherever power can be taken from the grid. This allows the machine to be used in mobile, stationary or mixed mode, which greatly increases its functionality and economic efficiency.

What does the franchisee actually buy and sell?

By buying an ECOES MultiCharger, the franchisee does not buy a machine. They buy a real, high-revenue business that they can develop according to their own vision and means. They do not sell electricity. They sell their customers the security and peace of mind that they will not be stranded on the road with an empty battery. Security and peace of mind are not goods: they are a state that is not obtained with money, but with help given at a critical moment.

ECOES Franchise does not offer you a business idea. It offers you a certified, working business with the hardware and software support without which a quick and smooth entry into this business is impossible.

That is why ECOES Franchise is the door through which you can enter the energy business.
