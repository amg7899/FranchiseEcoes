# Image kit (prompts for the client's ChatGPT round)

Built 2026-11-03 for the client, who regenerates the site's images with ChatGPT for maximum quality. Each slot: the original from the visualisation PDF v5, a site screenshot with the slot framed in red, and the prompt below. When the images come back: check product fidelity (hatches, chargers, lettering, frame, wheels) against the original; products → `python3 tools/cutout.py <file> <name>` (white background → alpha, WebP sizes); hero → crop 16:9 with the MultiCharger at ~60 % width, export 800/1200/1672/2400 WebP + JPEG, rebuild `amb-beach` (blur 34, brightness .5) and `og-image.jpg` from it; embed provenance with `impeccable embed-prompt` ("Generated by the client with ChatGPT from …, prompt in tools/image-kit.md").

## 01-home-hero – Home – main picture (full screen)

Where: Home, the first screen: full screen, behind the glass panel with the headline.

Reference files: `1-original.png` + `2-site.png`

```text
Image 1 is the current main picture of our website – its resolution is too low. Image 2 is a screenshot of the website: the red frame shows where the picture is used – full screen, behind a frosted-glass panel with the headline on the left.

Context: this picture is for franchise.ecoes.eu, the website of ECOES Franchise (ECOES JV) – a franchise for mobile DC fast-charging stations for electric vehicles. The website has a dark, calm, premium design: a near-black navy background, frosted-glass panels, thin glowing blue lines and light, wide typography.

Create a new, high-resolution version of the same scene: the red ECOES MultiCharger on its two-axle trailer, towed by the white pickup, charging three cars with its three DC chargers (no brand badges on any vehicle); the red vending machine beside it; the blue WC sign; the café with white umbrellas and a few guests; palm trees, the beach and the sea behind.

The ECOES MultiCharger is a real product (its design is registered and it is being certified), and customers pre-order it from these pictures – so it must stay exactly as in the reference: the same shape and proportions, the same signal-red body, the galvanised steel frame with its rivets and corner pieces, the same number and position of hatches, the black DC chargers with a blue ring light and their black cables, and the white lettering "ECOES MultiCharger" – spelled exactly like this, in the same place and style. Do not add, remove, move or redesign anything on it.

Light: blue hour, about 15 minutes after sunset – a deep blue sky with a last warm glow on the horizon, a dark blue sea, warm string lights in the café, the chargers' blue ring lights and the cars' rear lights glowing softly, gentle reflections on the paving stones.

Composition: landscape 16:9. The MultiCharger and the cars slightly right of centre (the MultiCharger centred at about 60 % of the width), whole, nothing cut off. Keep the left third calmer and darker (sea, palms, paving): the glass panel with the headline sits there. Leave some space above and below the main subject – the website crops the top and bottom on wide screens and shows only the middle part on phones. Full-frame camera, 35 mm lens, eye level (about 1.6 m). People small, in the background, natural, not looking at the camera.

Make it look like a real photograph by a professional commercial photographer, not like AI: natural light, true-to-life materials and colours (powder-coated and galvanised steel, rubber, glass, car paint, stone), fine real-world detail and small natural imperfections (a little dust, soft reflections, slight wear), correct perspective, believable shadows and reflections, a subtle film grain. Avoid: a plastic CGI look, oversaturated colours, HDR halos, over-sharpening, glowing outlines, lens flares, any extra text, logos, signs with invented words, watermarks or a frame.

Output: the largest landscape size you can make.
```
