Battery mobile charging station vs. ECOES MultiCharger Box

The economic reality in numbers

Get an offer

When investors and fleet operators hear about mobile DC fast charging, their first question is often: “Do you offer battery-powered mobile charging stations?” The answer is “NO” – because while mobile stations that rely solely on batteries may look good on paper, in real-world commercial conditions they result in losses from day one; in contrast, the MultiCharger Box generates high profits for you right from the very first day.

A mobile charging station is most effective about 200 km away from major cities, where high-capacity grid connections are unavailable. To deliver continuous daily output (1,440 kWh of DC energy per day at 50% utilization, or 2,160 kWh at 75%), you need a 40-foot battery container with a 2,000 kWh capacity weighing over 25–30 metric tons.

The catch is that one container is never enough. You need at least two containers (4,000 kWh total battery capacity) swapped every single day: while one unit is charging vehicles on-site, the second unit must be hauled 200 km back to the city (a 400 km daily round trip), spend hours recharging at an expensive high-power substation, and be hauled back again.

The bottom line for your business: If instead of buying a single battery-container mobile charging setup at €1,200,000 (plus €200,000/$225,000 per year in tractor diesel, driver wages, maintenance, and insurance), you invest the exact same amount in 12 ECOES MultiCharger Box stations, your gross monthly profit will reach approximately €311,000 ($350,000) per month at 50% utilization. This allows you to achieve full payback in about 5–6 months – after which the ECOES MultiCharger Box turns into a daily cash generator feeding directly into your bank account: over €3,700,000 ($4,180,000) per year at 50% utilization, without hauling 30-ton containers back and forth on the highway.
