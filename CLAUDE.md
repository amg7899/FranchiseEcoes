# FRANCHISE ECOES website

Static site (plain HTML + CSS + a little JS, no build step) for ECOES Franchise, the mobile EV-charging franchise of ECOES JV, split off from the ECOES JV site (https://ecoes.eu/, repo amg7899/SUM) at the client's request on 2026-10-15. Content source: the client's visualisation PDF "ECOES Franchise – визуализация на сайта" v5 (2026-11-03, 21 pages; made in this session from the client's PDF and the MultiCharger Box Word file) – texts, images, page structure. Published by GitHub Pages from `main` (root) at https://amg7899.github.io/FranchiseEcoes/ once the client enables Pages (Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → main, / (root)); planned domain https://franchise.ecoes.eu (DNS at SuperHosting: CNAME franchise → amg7899.github.io, then Custom domain in Pages settings; when it is live add `rel=canonical` + hreflang alternates with absolute URLs to every page, and change the base in `404.html`). Repo is public.

## Working with the client
- The client writes in Bulgarian (sometimes English); answer in the language of their message.
- Use the client's text verbatim. Do not add copy, taglines or sections. Fix only obvious typos, and report every fix. When unsure, ask.
- After every change: commit, push to `main`, and send the live link plus screenshots.
- The client chose (2026-11-03): English + Bulgarian, static site like ecoes.eu (edits through Claude), the design left to us ("choose the best"). Brief: professional, modern, futuristic but true to the subject, clean, trustworthy, not looking AI-made; transparent containers with visible motifs; the strongest points on Home as linked containers, each with a "get offer" link to the contact form (as on ecoes.eu); only the logo is the same as ecoes.eu.

## Design system (see DESIGN.md) – v2 "night glass"
History: v1 (2026-11-03 morning, the impeccable roll's "special-equipment body": red panels in a galvanised frame, riveted plates, Sofia Sans Condensed) was rejected the same day: "made in Paint", "all red strains the eye", "too obvious". The client asked for nonchalant and mysterious, professional, modern, futuristic and clean, with much more transparency (blurred glass containers with motifs), and sent references they like: an eco dashboard on a glass monitor with a glowing globe and leaves, a dark engineering site with a glowing wireframe building, a cinematic dark car page. Do not bring the red body, rivets or Sofia Sans back.
- Ground: night #06090c (body::before adds faint sea and ember light). Text #edf2f4 / #c3ced5 / #8f9ca6 (measured ≥ 6.8:1 on the hero glass and the header over the photo).
- Glass `.glass`: tint rgba(14,21,28,.42) + 160° white sheen, `backdrop-filter: blur(28px) saturate(1.6)`, light gradient edge (`::before` mask), radius 28px (chips/tiles 20px), no drop shadow. Rule (client, 2026-11-03): a motif behind glass only where it means something – the hero scene, the product cut-outs of the offers, the hologram, the globe; never a random object peeking out behind a pane (the Box louvres, the road sign and the LPG container behind the profit, certification and pre-order panes and the Box behind the contact form were removed: "I don't see the logic"). Those panes stand in designed light (`.split-body::before`: the charger's blue and the sea, positions per section via `--l1`/`--l2`).
- Motifs: hero = full-bleed beach photo (graded darker to the left) behind the frosted pane and the key-figure chips; offers = product cut-outs standing behind the panes (sharp above, frosted below); "Why" = the wireframe MultiCharger, whole: it fits the viewport (`--wire-w: min(94vw, 148vh, 1480px)`, centre at `--wire-top` 57vh, 42vh on phones), the panes start below it (`.why .tiles` margin from the wire's height), so the visitor first sees the whole drawing under the title, then the panes slide over it; the sticky stage ends at the last row of panes (`.why-backdrop` bottom = section padding), so no wheels are left alone below them; on phones the stage is not sticky. Contact = blurred beach (`amb-beach`). Sections use `overflow-x: clip` (not hidden: sticky columns keep working).
- Cut-outs (`assets/img/v2/cut-*.webp`): the client's renders with the white background turned into alpha (colour-to-alpha, shadows become translucent black), WebP only. Line drawings (`assets/img/v2/wire-multicharger.svg`, `wire-globe.svg`) are generated by `tools/wireframes.py` (standard-library Python; edit the numbers there and rerun); they draw themselves once when they load; `main.js` loads them (`img.js-draw[data-src]`) only when 30 % is in view, so the visitor sees them drawn; a `<noscript>` copy shows them finished without JS (`.js-draw` is hidden until main.js adds `html.js`); finished under reduced motion.
- Colour: signal blue #4cc3ff (the charger's ring light) for the status dots of "Get an offer", the current page, the selected load/bar, radio/checkbox accents and the line drawings. Red only where the product is (photos, renders, logo). Primary button = ice pill; second = glass pill.
- Type: Unbounded light (display, figures) + Wix Madefor Text (text), both OFL, self-hosted in `assets/fonts/` (Latin + Cyrillic, `assets/css/fonts.css`). With `lang="bg"` both show the Bulgarian Cyrillic letterforms (OpenType locl; Unbounded changes в г д ж з и й к л п т ц ш щ ъ ь, Wix Madefor Text also ч and ю; in both the capitals Д Л Ф; Latin and digits stay). Shown the client both variants on 2026-11-03 (comparison board), their choice pending. To switch to the standard forms: `:lang(bg), :lang(bg) * { font-feature-settings: "locl" 0 !important; }` – it must reach every element, because the `font` shorthand in the component rules resets font-feature-settings.
- Motion: on load the hero pane frosts over the photo (backdrop blur 0 → 28px) while the photo settles; the chips follow; line drawings draw once. Nothing else moves on its own. Reduced motion respected.
- Signature interaction: the load meter on Home (10/25/50/75/100%) recomputes gross profit per day/month/year from the client's table (`main.js`, data in the table rows' `data-dc`/`data-service`); without JS the table shows.
- Quality floor: contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (sample the rendered pixels behind glass text, not the CSS), 2px ice focus outline, 44–52px targets, no horizontal scroll 320–1920 with a +45% Cyrillic pseudo-translation, header row of links collapses to the menu button when it does not fit (`html.nav-collapsed`).
- Screenshots: capture viewport by viewport while scrolling (a viewport as tall as the page breaks the `svh` hero and the sticky stage).

## Languages (English + Bulgarian)
- `bg/` mirrors every English page with the same markup; only the text differs and asset paths have one more `../`. Every change to an English page must be made on its `bg/` twin too. Links inside `bg/` stay inside `bg/`.
- Home and the forms: the client's English; their Bulgarian is ours. Franchise / MultiCharger / MultiCharger Box: the client's Bulgarian; English is ours. Contact us and the footer: from ecoes.eu (EN + BG).
- Numbers: English 24,600 EUR; Bulgarian as in the client's PDF, 24.600 EUR (24.600,00 in running text), decimals with a comma (0,60 EUR/kWh).
- Names stay in Latin and carry `translate="no"` (ECOES Franchise, ECOES MultiCharger, ECOES MultiCharger Box, ECOES JV, company names, addresses, e-mails, phones), with neighbouring spaces inside the span.
- `main.js`: the language switch keeps `?query#hash`; a language chosen with the switch is kept for the tab (sessionStorage `ecoes-franchise-lang`) and marks its pages `translate="no"`; when the browser translates into a language the site has, it goes to our page instead.

## Forms
- `assets/js/forms.js` handles every `form.js-form`: required fields checked next to them (text/e-mail/checkbox; required radio groups via `fieldset.choice` + its `aria-describedby`), first invalid field focused, messages by `data-lang` (en/bg). Sent by FormSubmit AJAX to `https://formsubmit.co/ajax/sales@ecoes.eu`, `_cc=bulfire@ecoes.eu`, `_template=table`, honeypot `_honey`, subject from `data-subject` (+ ": " + topic on Contact). Without JS it posts normally and returns with `?sent=1`. On failure: "not activated yet" or the general error + details, with a mailto link carrying the entries.
- FormSubmit needs a one-time activation per site: the first submission from the live site sends "Activate Form" to sales@ecoes.eu (still to do once Pages is live).
- Pre-order form (Home; later also MultiCharger and MultiCharger Box pages, as the visualisation asks): the client's 8 fields; field 2 and 3 split into inputs (phone/WhatsApp, e-mail*, website; country*, city, postal code); 4, 5, 7, 8 as choices; required: name, e-mail, country, type, consent. Consent text as on ecoes.eu.
- Contact form (`contact-us/#contact-form`): name*, company, e-mail*, phone, country, subject (`?topic=` franchise, multicharger, multicharger-box, other), message*, consent*. Every "Get an offer" link goes to `contact-us/?topic=…#contact-form`.
