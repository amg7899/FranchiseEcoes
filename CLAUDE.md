# FRANCHISE ECOES website

Static site (plain HTML + CSS + a little JS, no build step) for ECOES Franchise, the mobile EV-charging franchise of ECOES JV, split off from the ECOES JV site (https://ecoes.eu/, repo amg7899/SUM) at the client's request on 2026-10-15. Content source: the client's visualisation PDF "ECOES Franchise – визуализация на сайта" v5 (2026-11-03, 21 pages; made in this session from the client's PDF and the MultiCharger Box Word file) – texts, images, page structure. Published by GitHub Pages from `main` (root) at https://amg7899.github.io/FranchiseEcoes/ once the client enables Pages (Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → main, / (root)); planned domain https://franchise.ecoes.eu (DNS at SuperHosting: CNAME franchise → amg7899.github.io, then Custom domain in Pages settings; when it is live add `rel=canonical` + hreflang alternates with absolute URLs to every page, and change the base in `404.html`). Repo is public.

## Working with the client
- The client writes in Bulgarian (sometimes English); answer in the language of their message.
- Use the client's text verbatim. Do not add copy, taglines or sections. Fix only obvious typos, and report every fix. When unsure, ask.
- After every change: commit, push to `main`, and send the live link plus screenshots.
- The client chose (2026-11-03): English + Bulgarian, static site like ecoes.eu (edits through Claude), the design left to us ("choose the best"). Brief: professional, modern, futuristic but true to the subject, clean, trustworthy, not looking AI-made; transparent containers with visible motifs; the strongest points on Home as linked containers, each with a "get offer" link to the contact form (as on ecoes.eu); only the logo is the same as ecoes.eu.

## Design system (see DESIGN.md)
World: the ECOES MultiCharger's own equipment body (chosen by the impeccable roll from our own list: candidate 6, "special-equipment body": BULFIRE fire/airport vehicles + the product's red body). Every section is one of four objects on a daylight steel ground:
- Red body `.panel`: #c8161d, galvanised frame drawn as box-shadow rings, rivets along top/bottom (`::before`), two amber marker lights at the top corners (`::after`). Hero, "Why ECOES Franchise", Announcements, page heads of inner pages, the menu. One red mass per viewport.
- Compartment `.locker`: clear window (`.locker-media`, `aspect-ratio` via `--ar`; renders on white use `.locker-media--fit` = `object-fit: contain`) + frosted panel `.locker-glass` (white 80%, blur 14px) overlapping the window's lower 56px – the client's "transparent containers with visible motifs". Title, one paragraph, then "Learn more" + "Get an offer" (`.locker-links`, pushed to the bottom).
- Plate `.plate`: brushed aluminium with four rivets – key figures, certification, load meter, forms, offices.
- Hatch `.hatch`: clear glass with gasket ring and reflection – the hero photo.
- Type: Sofia Sans + Sofia Sans Condensed (Lettersoup, Sofia; OFL; self-hosted in `assets/fonts/`, Latin + Cyrillic subsets, `assets/css/fonts.css`). Condensed 700 = vehicle lettering (hero "ECOES MultiCharger" up to 140px). Sofia Sans' Cyrillic defaults to the Bulgarian letterforms (д, т, в as in handwriting); `lang="ru"` gives standard forms (so Chrome's Russian translation shows them); offered to the client.
- Motion: one light sweep along the hero's contour strip on load; meter bar transition; nothing on scroll. Reduced motion respected.
- Signature interaction: the load meter on Home (10/25/50/75/100%) recomputes gross profit per day/month/year from the client's table (`main.js`, data in the table rows' `data-dc`/`data-service`); without JS the table shows.
- Quality floor: contrast ≥ 4.5:1, 2px focus outline (white on red), 44–52px targets, no horizontal scroll 320–1920 with a +45% Cyrillic pseudo-translation (test script approach: replace text nodes, check `scrollWidth` and elements past the viewport), header row of links collapses to the menu button when it does not fit (`html.nav-collapsed`, re-checked on resize, font load, lang change, text change).

## Languages (English + Bulgarian)
- `bg/` mirrors every English page with the same markup; only the text differs and asset paths have one more `../`. Every change to an English page must be made on its `bg/` twin too. Links inside `bg/` stay inside `bg/`.
- Home and the forms: the client's English; their Bulgarian is ours. Franchise / MultiCharger / MultiCharger Box: the client's Bulgarian; English is ours. Contact us and the footer: from ecoes.eu (EN + BG).
- Numbers: English 24,600 EUR; Bulgarian as in the client's PDF, 24.600 EUR (24.600,00 in running text), decimals with a comma (0,60 EUR/kWh).
- Names stay in Latin and carry `translate="no"` (ECOES Franchise, ECOES MultiCharger, ECOES MultiCharger Box, ECOES JV, company names, addresses, e-mails, phones), with neighbouring spaces inside the span.
- `main.js`: the language switch keeps `?query#hash`; a language chosen with the switch is kept for the tab (sessionStorage `ecoes-franchise-lang`) and marks its pages `translate="no"`; when the browser translates into a language the site has, it goes to our page instead.

## Forms
- `assets/js/forms.js` handles every `form.js-form`: required fields checked next to them (text/e-mail/checkbox; required radio groups via `fieldset.choice` + its `aria-describedby`), first invalid field focused, messages by `data-lang` (en/bg). Sent by FormSubmit AJAX to `https://formsubmit.co/ajax/sales@ecoes.eu`, `_cc=bulfire@ecoes.eu`, `_template=table`, honeypot `_honey`, subject from `data-subject` (+ ": " + topic on Contact). Without JS it posts normally and returns with `?sent=1`. On failure: "not activated yet" or the general error + details, with a mailto link carrying the entries.
- FormSubmit needs a one-time activation per site: the first submission from the live site sends "Activate Form" to sales@ecoes.eu (still to do once Pages is live).
- Pre-order form (Home; later also MultiCharger and MultiCharger Box pages, as the visualisation asks): the client's 8 fields; field 2 and 3 split into inputs (phone/WhatsApp, e-mail*, website; country*, city, postal code); 4, 5, 7, 8 as choices; required: name, e-mail, country, type, consent. Consent text as on ecoes.eu.
- Contact form (`contact-us/#contact-form`): name*, company, e-mail*, phone, country, subject (`?topic=` franchise, multicharger, multicharger-box, other), message*, consent*. Every "Get an offer" link goes to `contact-us/?topic=…#contact-form`.
