Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who is responsible for your data

BULFIRE ECOES Ltd., 205 Al. Stamboliyski Blvd., Office 320A, Sofia 1309, Bulgaria, EU, part of ECOES JV, is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: ecoes@ecoes.eu

What data we collect

  • Data you send through the forms on this website (contact, pre-order and dealer application): your name, company, e-mail, phone or WhatsApp number, website, country, city, postal code, your message and the choices you make in the form.
  • Data you send through a lead form in our LinkedIn ads: your name, e-mail, company, job title, country and your answer to our question.

We collect no other personal data on this website.

Why we use it

To answer your inquiry, to send you the information you asked for (for example the presentation), and to handle your pre-order or dealer application. We do this on the basis of your consent, which you give in the form, and to take the steps you asked for before a possible contract (Art. 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR). We do not sell your data or use it for anything else.

Who receives it

  • Website forms are delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) as e-mails to sales@ecoes.eu and ecoes@ecoes.eu.
  • LinkedIn lead forms are stored by LinkedIn, and we download them from LinkedIn Campaign Manager.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the pages.

Some of these services are based outside the EU, for example in the USA. Inside ECOES JV only the people who handle inquiries see your data.

Cookies

This website uses no cookies for tracking or advertising and no visitor analytics. It only remembers your language choice in your browser.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it to handle your inquiry and any business that follows from it, and as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, restrict or object to its use, and receive it in a portable format. You can withdraw your consent at any time; this does not affect what was done before. Write to ecoes@ecoes.eu. You can also complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (www.cpdp.bg).
