Five main certification pillars

The full certification process includes the five main certification pillars, plus additional standards and certificates.

Sales of the ECOES MultiCharger will begin in 2027, following the completion of the certification process.

  • Automotive standard

    Type approval for full compliance with EU and UN requirements for bodywork and rescue vehicles (ready European CoC for immediate registration).

  • Machinery Directive

    Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230. EN ISO 8528-13: the specific safety standard for generating sets driven by internal combustion engines.

  • Energy standard

    Charging architecture under LVD 2014/35/EU: EN IEC 61851-1, EN IEC 61851-23 and EN IEC 61851-24 (protocols ISO 15118 and DIN 70121).

  • Electromagnetic compatibility (EMC)

    EMC Directive 2014/30/EU: EN 61000-6-2 / EN 61000-6-4, immunity and emission of interference in industrial environments.

  • Outdoor noise level

    Outdoor Noise Directive 2000/14/EC: the guaranteed sound power level.
