# Home – surface brief

Scope: Home (`index.html`, twin `bg/index.html`), visitor mode Persuade. The same world carries every later page of the site.

Audience and job: franchise prospects (entrepreneurs, investors, roadside businesses in Europe, Africa, Asia, Latin America) judging whether ECOES Franchise is a real, certified, profitable business, then pre-ordering for 2027 or asking for an offer.

Proof on hand: the client's renders of the red MultiCharger and the Box, the beach charging scene, the key figures, the profit table at 10–100% load, the certification pillars, the announcements and the 8-field pre-order form (all from the visualisation PDF v5). Nothing invented: no customers, prices, testimonials.

Constraints: client's text verbatim (BG); EN of the BG pages is our translation; "Get an offer" link on every product link to `contact-us/?topic=…#contact-form`; transparent containers with visible motifs (client's brief); logo as on ecoes.eu; translation-proof; no cookie banner.

Candidate list (ordered by resonance):
1. See-through shipping container (the product ships in, and is, a container).
2. Motorway signage system (the EV sign and the 50–100 m distance boards it ships with).
3. Rating plates and engineering drawings (certification, dimension lines).
4. Fuel-station forecourt totem (price per kWh, canopy light).
5. Overland expedition map (remote routes, range rings).
6. Special-equipment body: BULFIRE fire and airport vehicles and the MultiCharger's own body (red panels, galvanised frame, roller shutters, glazed hatches, marker lights).
7. Grid-operations telemetry (remote monitoring, network map).
