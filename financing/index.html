Financing, insurance and investment protection

Get an offer

When you buy a MultiCharger or MultiCharger Box, ECOES Franchise gives its franchisees and dealers full support in structuring the financing and minimising the investment risk:

  1. International financial leasing (for customers in Europe)

    We provide direct assistance with leading international leasing companies in obtaining financial leasing. Approval and the specific terms are set by the lessor on the basis of the franchisee’s creditworthiness.

  2. Direct instalment payment without leasing approval (in Europe and worldwide)

    For customers in Europe and beyond, we offer direct instalment payment of 50% of the value of the equipment over 12 to 24 months — with no need for bank or leasing approval. With the real returns of the MultiCharger and MultiCharger Box, which allow the investment to pay back in full in about 5–6 months, this period ensures comfortable cash flow and a net profit already while the instalments are being paid.

  3. Property and financial insurance

    We provide assistance through international insurance brokers and companies in issuing all types of property (material) and financial (lease and credit) insurance. The terms of the financial insurance depend on the insurer and the buyer’s credit profile.

  4. Guaranteed buy-back protection

    For franchisees who, for whatever reason, decide to end their activity or do not manage to develop their site, we provide an option to buy back the MultiCharger and MultiCharger Box at 50% of the purchase price (valid between the 1st and the 3rd year from the date of purchase). Buy-back is possible only once the franchisee has paid the full purchase price, including all instalments or lease payments.

The specific financial parameters, schedules and terms are set individually in the Purchase Offer and the Franchise Agreement.

We do not offer you just a business idea.

We offer you a real, high-revenue and certified business that starts generating income in your bank account from the first day you receive your MultiCharger or MultiCharger Box!
